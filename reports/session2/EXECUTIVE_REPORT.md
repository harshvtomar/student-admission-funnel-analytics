# CampusGrowth Analytics — management review

Portfolio simulation. Fictional institutions and students; no affiliation with KollegeApply. Acquisition: January–December 2025; outcome snapshot: 31 December 2025.

The operation spent ₹18,938,788.13 against ₹21,180,497 allocated, or 89.4% utilization. Its 50,000 unique leads produced 13,792 completed applications and 5,627 confirmed enrollments. Observed lead-to-enrollment conversion was 11.254%; cost per completed application was ₹1,373.17 and cost per enrollment was ₹3,365.70. Costs include simulated operating costs for organic, referral, email and webinar acquisition.

Application progression deserves operational review: 20,115 starts produced 13,792 completions, leaving 6,323 incomplete at the snapshot. This is a follow-up queue, not a final abandonment estimate, because recent students remain right-censored.

Prioritize C007 for investigation. In its mature cohort, 349 leads produced 32 enrollments; spend was ₹251,126.99, CPE ₹7,847.72 and completed-application conversion 18.3%, versus the pooled mature benchmark of 29.1%. Audit targeting, qualification and application follow-up, then test a revised message with a randomized control. Historical differences do not establish causality.

The default ₹10 lakh budget plan allocates 25% each to Referral, Education webinars and Organic Search, 10% to Google Search and 5% each to Email, Meta Ads and Display Ads. This is the bounded linear solution under constant mature historical CPE, a 5% minimum and 25% maximum per channel. Modeled enrollment equivalents are 302.2, 377.8 and 444.5 under assumed cost multipliers 1.25, 1.00 and 0.85. No model estimates actual future or incremental returns. Validate capacity and incremental response before using these shares operationally.

Institution reviews should combine volume, conversion and cost: larger enrollments may reflect more spend or a different cohort mix. Geographic comparisons have no allocated delivery costs and cannot support real market-demand claims. Use the exported client packs for scoped campaign discussions and the Excel controls for consistent management reporting.

Recommended cadence: weekly campaign and follow-up investigations; monthly SQL/Python/Excel reconciliation and institutional reviews; controlled tests before major budget changes. Real CRM integration, consent controls and delivery-grain costs are prerequisites for production use.
